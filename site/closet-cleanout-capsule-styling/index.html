Book a Free ConsultationView Pricing
Overview

How we can help

Clear out the clutter in your wardrobe and make getting dressed effortless every single day. We start by editing what no longer fits your body or lifestyle, then shop intentionally for a versatile, mix-and-match collection of colors and styles that multiply your outfit options.

What's included

Complimentary 30-Minute Discovery CallA quick chat to discuss your personal style goals, professional image needs, and whether virtual or in-person support works best for you.
Virtual or In-Person Closet CleanoutStep-by-step support—either via video call or hands-on in your home (available locally in the Houston area)—to sort through your wardrobe and decide what to keep, tailor, donate, or let go.
Personal Color, Style & Image BlueprintA tailored guide to the silhouettes, fabrics, and color palette that flatter you best and align with your personal and professional brand.
Online or In-Person Capsule & Accessory ShoppingChoose between curated shoppable links or a guided personal shopping trip together to select versatile clothing, shoes, bags, and accessories that pull every look together.
Complete Mix-and-Match Outfit LookbookVisual outfit combinations—down to the finishing accessories—showing you how to style your pieces for work, interviews, leadership roles, weekends, and special events.
