Overview

How we can help

Strategic career and enrollment support tailored to both individual job seekers and adult education partners:

  • For Individuals: Stand out in today's job market with polished resume writing, targeted interview preparation, job search strategy, and confident career-change guidance.
  • For Training Providers & Organizations: Strengthen student recruitment, community outreach, and enrollment pipelines for workforce certification programs.

Moving to the US or Canada? As an immigrant and internationally trained professional myself, I help newcomers present their international experience in a resume North American employers understand, prepare for local interview styles, and build a job search plan for their new market.
