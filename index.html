Olga Demina Founder · Coach & Consultant
Professional bio

Most people don’t need ten different specialists - they need one trusted partner who sees the whole picture. I started Xtraordinari Life to be that person. I bring coaching, consulting, and practical services together in a confidential, non-judgmental space so every part of your life moves in the same direction.

My approach isn't just based on theory; it comes from formal training, real-world expertise, and personal transformation. As an immigrant and internationally trained professional, I know firsthand what it takes to adapt, rebuild confidence, and thrive in a new environment.

Today, I am also continuing my studies in nutrition school, inspired by my own personal journey of losing significant weight and embracing anti-aging nutrition through targeted foods and sustainable eating habits.

Whether you are navigating a career pivot, building confidence in English, refreshing your wardrobe and personal image, or fueling your body for lasting vitality, I meet you right where you are - with empathy, practical tools, and zero judgment.
